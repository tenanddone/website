Your list has no end. Your day does.

You can never finish a list with no end. Ten notes, you can. That’s why Ten & Done has exactly ten spots: your day gets a goal you can really reach.

Why not more?

Because otherwise every evening ends with things still open. Nothing gets lost: everything else waits in the parking spot, or you plan it for another day. If something new comes up today, it becomes an extra.

Why not fewer?

Because a day isn’t just three big tasks. With ten, the small things get their place too. Events and habits count, and a glass of water counts like your taxes.

Why always exactly ten?

Because then you don’t have to work out each morning how much is enough. Ten out of ten means: finished, day done. Today, tomorrow, every day.

How it works

How your day gets its structure

Four steps, the same every day. After a week you won’t even think about it.

  1. Write

    In the evening or in the morning: ten notes on your board, big and small. Type them or say them.

  2. Commit

    Pick your top task and press and hold the button. Your plan is set.

  3. Check off

    Every finished note gets its stamp. Anything new becomes an extra.

  4. Wrap up

    In the evening you decide: move to tomorrow, later, or let it go.

FAQ

Good to know

Why exactly ten?

Because a list with no end is never finished. Ten spots give every day the same goal: ten out of ten means done. Ten fit on one screen, everything else waits in the parking spot, and notes can be as small as today allows.

What if I can’t think of ten things?

Then pick small ones. “Drink a glass of water” counts just like “do my taxes”. Tap 💡 and Tack suggests something: things you’ve done before, items from your parking spot and small ideas.

What happens if I don’t finish something?

Nothing. The note just waits in the parking spot, and the next morning Tack asks whether it should go back on the board. If it keeps getting left, Tack suggests splitting it into small steps.

I’m sick or on vacation. Now what?

Mark the days. Then there’s no plan and no reminders. Your streak waits until you’re back.

Can I do more than ten things?

Yes. After you commit, anything new becomes an extra. Extras don’t earn points, but you’ll see them in your progress.

Does Ten & Done cost anything?

The core is free: 10 tasks a day, habits, Focus Time, calendar, lists, notes, widget and the progress of your week. Premium is a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase.

Is there a widget?

Yes. The board widget shows your daily plan on your home screen, in the style of your board, and you can check things off right there. Tap the mic in the widget to add new tasks by voice.

Can the app see my calendar?

Only if you turn it on. Then it shows events from your phone’s calendar as notes. When you create an event in the app, it adds it to your calendar – Google or Apple. It doesn’t change other events.

Does it work on tablets?

Yes, in portrait and landscape. On a tablet, timeline and lists sit side by side and progress is laid out in columns.

Is Ten & Done available for Android and iPhone?

Yes. Ten & Done is available for Android phones and tablets and for iPhone and iPad.
